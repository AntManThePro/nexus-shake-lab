# NEXUS // SHAKE LAB v2.2

Local mixologist. Pick a goal, tap ingredients, blend, save your shake formula. No cloud, no account, no API call — everything runs on your device and your recipes live in your browser.

**Live:** https://nexus-shake-lab.netlify.app/

## How to use

1. Pick a goal — muscle, keto, energy, or whatever you're chasing today.
2. Tap ingredients from the 22 in the matrix. Macros add up live as you tap.
3. Hit BLEND, watch it mix, then SAVE if it's a keeper. Saved formulas load back anytime.
4. Stuck? Ask the local mixologist — it answers from the ingredient table in the file, not a cloud model.

## Notes

- 22 ingredients, real macro math, saves under `nexus-shake-lab-recipes` in localStorage.
- Works offline once loaded. Your recipes stay on your device.
